📊 Key findings

The important results are visible directly on this portfolio — no PDF required.

⏱️ Delivery Time
62% longer
Cancelled vs delivered

Cancelled orders take 62% longer to resolve than delivered ones. This points to cancellation timing, fulfilment delays and customer-service touchpoints as areas to investigate.

📍 Regional Delays
5 states
Highest delivery times

RR, AP, AM, AL and PA were identified as the clearest starting points for a logistics review.

⭐ Product Experience
22.9%
Fashion low-rating share

Fashion and audio categories showed the highest share of low ratings. These categories warrant deeper checks on quality, seller performance and listing accuracy.

💳 Revenue Mix
78%
Credit-card revenue share

Credit card drives 78% of total revenue, making payment reliability and checkout conversion important areas to monitor.
