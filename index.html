01 · Delivery Performance

Why are some orders taking so long to resolve?

SQL analysis
SELECT order_status, COUNT(order_id) AS total_orders, ROUND(AVG(DATEDIFF(order_delivered_customer_date, order_purchase_timestamp)), 1) AS avg_delivery_days FROM olist_orders_dataset WHERE order_status IN ('delivered','canceled') GROUP BY order_status ORDER BY avg_delivery_days DESC;
Why it matters
Cancelled orders take materially longer to resolve, so cancellation timing, fulfilment delays and customer-service touchpoints should be investigated.

02 · Regional Performance

Which regions need logistics investment first?

SQL analysis
SELECT customer_state, COUNT(order_id) AS total_orders, ROUND(AVG(DATEDIFF( order_delivered_customer_date, order_purchase_timestamp)), 1) AS avg_delivery_days FROM olist_orders_dataset o JOIN olist_customers_dataset c ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id WHERE o.order_status = 'delivered' GROUP BY customer_state ORDER BY avg_delivery_days DESC;
Why it matters
Start the logistics review with the states showing the highest average delivery times.

03 · Product Experience

Which product categories are underperforming with customers?

SQL analysis
SELECT product_category_name, COUNT(DISTINCT review_id) AS total_reviews, ROUND(AVG(review_score), 2) AS avg_review_score, ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN review_score <= 2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS low_score_pct FROM reviews + products GROUP BY product_category_name HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT review_id) >= 30 ORDER BY low_score_pct DESC;
Why it matters
Categories with a high share of low ratings warrant deeper checks on product quality, seller performance and listing accuracy.

04 · Revenue Mix

Where does the business actually make its revenue?

SQL analysis
SELECT payment_type, COUNT(payment_value) AS transactions, ROUND(AVG(payment_value), 2) AS avg_payment_value, ROUND(SUM(payment_value), 2) AS total_revenue FROM olist_order_payments_dataset GROUP BY payment_type ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
Why it matters
Revenue is concentrated in one payment channel, so payment reliability and checkout conversion deserve ongoing monitoring.
