# Olist E-Commerce Business Analysis

### SQL + Advanced Excel | Business Analytics Portfolio

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses a 100k+ record e-commerce dataset to answer practical business questions around delivery performance, regional fulfilment, customer experience and revenue.

The objective was to move from raw transactional data to actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 🔍 Business Questions

### 1. Delivery Performance
Why are some orders taking longer to resolve?

### 2. Regional Performance
Which regions require greater logistics attention?

### 3. Product & Customer Experience
Which product categories have a higher share of low customer ratings?

### 4. Revenue Mix
Which payment methods contribute most to total revenue?

## 📊 Key Findings

- Cancelled orders took 62% longer to resolve than delivered orders.
- Five states were identified as the highest-delay regions in the analysis.
- Fashion had a 22.9% share of low customer ratings in the analysis.
- Credit-card payments accounted for 78% of total revenue.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Investigate cancellation journeys and fulfilment delays.
- Review logistics performance in high-delay regions.
- Investigate product quality, seller performance and listing accuracy in categories with higher low-rating shares.
- Monitor payment-channel performance and customer checkout experience.
