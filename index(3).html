🔎 What did I investigate?

Four practical business questions, analysed from raw e-commerce data.

1. Delivery PerformanceWhy are some orders taking longer to resolve?
2. Regional PerformanceWhich regions require greater logistics attention?
3. Product & Customer ExperienceWhich product categories have a higher share of low customer ratings?
4. Revenue MixWhich payment methods contribute most to total revenue?

📊 Key Findings

The key insights are available directly on this page — no PDF required.

⏱️ Delivery Performance
62%
Fulfilment insight

Cancelled orders took 62% longer to resolve than delivered orders.

Business meaning: cancellation journeys and fulfilment processes deserve investigation to understand where delays are being introduced.

📍 Regional Performance
5 states
Logistics insight

Five states were identified as the highest-delay regions in the analysis.

Business meaning: these regions should be reviewed for logistics capacity, carrier performance and fulfilment constraints.

⭐ Product & Customer Experience
22.9%
Customer experience

Fashion had a 22.9% share of low customer ratings in the analysis.

Business meaning: investigate product quality, seller performance and listing accuracy to understand the drivers of dissatisfaction.

💳 Revenue Mix
78%
Revenue insight

Credit-card payments accounted for 78% of total revenue.

Business meaning: payment-channel performance and the customer checkout experience should be monitored closely.
